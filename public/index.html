We’re Thunder Buddies Studios.
A team built around making things together.

Our story started in game communities. Working alongside players taught us to listen, experiment, and keep improving. That experience still shapes everything we build.

Today, our focus spans Roblox experiences, our own original game, independent platforms, and work for clients. Our open-source Arma library keeps a part of that history available for others to build on.

PROJECT ENQUIRIES

Your details.
Handled with care.

The request form collects your name, email, project brief, selected services, budget, timeline, and any optional company or reference link you provide. Thunder Buddies Studios uses these details to assess your enquiry and contact you about the work.

Requests are stored privately on the studio’s hosting service and are accessible to the studio through an authenticated inbox. Form entries are not published on this website. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or confidential project files.

To ask about an enquiry or request removal of your details, contact the studio through our community channel and quote your request reference. Do not post your full brief or personal details in a public channel.

This site does not use advertising trackers. Its offline feature caches public site files on your device; it does not cache submitted requests.
